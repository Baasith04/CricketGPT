# IPL 2024 Final Context (KKR vs SRH)

Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) won their third IPL championship in 2024, defeating Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH) in the final at MA Chidambaram Stadium (Chepauk), Chennai on May 26, 2024. Led by Shreyas Iyer with Gautam Gambhir returning as team mentor, KKR dominated the tournament with a balanced bowling attack featuring Mitchell Starc, Sunil Narine, Andre Russell, Varun Chakaravarthy, and Harshit Rana. In the final, KKR's bowlers dismantled SRH's aggressive batting lineup early in the powerplay and chased down the modest target comfortably inside 11 overs, capping one of the most one-sided finals in IPL history.

# Chennai Super Kings (CSK) IPL 2024 Campaign Overview

IPL 2024 marked a leadership transition for Chennai Super Kings as Ruturaj Gaikwad took over the captaincy from MS Dhoni ahead of the season opener. CSK finished with a balanced win-loss record across 14 league matches (7 wins and 7 losses) and narrowly missed qualifying for the playoffs on Net Run Rate after losing a decisive final league-stage fixture against Royal Challengers Bengaluru in Bengaluru. CSK also dealt with mid-season bowling absences (including Mustafizur Rahman's international departure and Matheesha Pathirana's injury spell).

# Virat Kohli vs Rohit Sharma in IPL 2024 (Role & Style Contrast)

Although both Virat Kohli (Royal Challengers Bengaluru) and Rohit Sharma (Mumbai Indians) opened the batting for their respective franchises in IPL 2024, their tactical roles differed significantly:
- **Virat Kohli** anchored RCB's innings with high consistency and volume across the entire season, winning the Orange Cap while accelerating noticeably against both pace and spin after earlier criticism of his middle-overs scoring rate.
- **Rohit Sharma** operated primarily as a high-intent powerplay aggressor for Mumbai Indians, prioritizing rapid early starts and boundary hitting rather than batting deep into the death overs.
